01 · EXPERIMENTAL DESIGN

One source.
Six operating points.
No moving goalposts.

The sequence was selected from source-only motion features before any retargeter ran. All outputs use the same 29-DoF G1 evaluator, the same coordinate contract, and an explicit completion threshold. The precomputed reference is kept in a separate evidentiary role.

CONTROLLED COMPARISON

Sparse and Dense differ only in their declared target set.

Solver, limits, scaling, warm start, iteration budget, damping, posture regularization, and initialization are held constant for Sparse/Dense. GMR, OmniRetarget, and ProtoMotions v3 retain frozen public formulations through isolated adapters. ProtoMotions v2.3 is explicitly a canonical-LAFAN benchmark port of its public G1 Mink tasks and solver, because upstream v2.3 does not publish a native LAFAN input path.

Scope boundary

This is a single-sequence Pilot—not a dataset-level ranking. Interaction evidence covers exactly two cases.

02 · QUALITY × SPEED

The frontier is not
a finish line.

Choose a pose or root-trajectory lens. Every point remains a separate operating point; lower and further left is better. Circle area encodes aggregate artifact rate.

Area = artifact rate

RF-KPE removes root translation and heading. “Common scale” uses one neutral-G1/source calibration for all methods; “native scale” evaluates each public method against its declared policy; “path shape” fits one scalar and isolates trajectory geometry.

03 · SCALE AS AN INTERVENTION

Same person.
Same robot.
Different target policy.

Robot morphology is fixed. The experiment perturbs root-path and local-landmark scale by ±5% before each native solver, while a same-solver Dense transplant isolates policy from optimizer choice.

Native solver response · fixed canonical contact labels

The formal causal arm freezes canonical contact labels. Holosoma's separately reported native-contact recomputation is a robustness analysis, because changing labels changes more than scale.

04 · EXTERNAL REFERENCE CORPUS

A useful benchmark.
Not an oracle.

The pinned Hugging Face corpus is attributed to Unitree and shares this Pilot's basename and 600 source-frame indices after one rigid coordinate conversion. Its nominal 30 Hz declaration is not the source's exact timestamp grid, and its uploader does not provide enough method provenance to establish official ground truth or a quality upper bound.

07 · INTERACTION ABLATION

What changes when
hard constraints unlock?

Full enables non-penetration and foot sticking. No-Hard disables only those two gates; joint limits, input, initialization, solver, and iteration budget remain fixed.

The primary penetration rate counts depth >1.1 mm: the frozen 1 mm constraint tolerance plus a 0.1 mm numerical margin. “Any negative” remains visible separately.
